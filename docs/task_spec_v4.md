# Finite-domain composition task, v4 development proposal

Date: 2026-10-09. Status: **closed prospective design for one development-only probe; no v4 candidates sampled and no feasibility PASS**. The v3 attempt remains NO-GO and unchanged. This design does not amend historical results, pass G2, or register model training. Its small machine-readable contract is [constraint_probe_v4.json](../configs/tasks/constraint_probe_v4.json).

## Research scope and authority

The eventual question remains whether one workspace and decoder interface trained once on a shared source mixture transfers to fixed-N unseen compositions and structures, comparing exchangeable (EX) and indexed (IDX) workspaces under matched decoder access. The v4 candidate changes the generated population and finite proposal before every later method; all comparisons must use the same eventual frozen v4 corpus. Preserve the residual update `Z_next = Z + F(LN(Z), X)`, iid slot noise, IDX identities added once at initialization, prompt-only answer-isolated `X`, and a frozen shared target interface. These architecture details do not authorize implementation or training now.

This proposal is subordinate to the v2.2 protocol and preserves the `constraint-task-v1` domain `{0,1,2}`, modulus 3, `eq`, `neq`, and `add_mod` semantics, strict verifier, prompt/answer boundary, independent exhaustive oracle, deterministic solver order, canonical identities, content and structure split hashes, eligibility predicates, and original numeric suitability floors. Existing v1/v2/v3 files, configs, run artifacts and claims remain immutable. See the v2 specification for full primitive formulas, normalization, canonical serialization, strict answer syntax, and the unchanged gates; v4-specific fixed choices and denominators below take precedence only for this proposal.

## Populations, cells and quotas

Every candidate has `n=6`. The four fixed strata are:

| Cell | Given count `g` | Constraint count `c` | Existing prompt-byte bin | Existing first-solution-node bin |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| A | 2 | 8 | 4: >1,023 UTF-8 bytes | 2: 4–7 nodes |
| B | 3 | 6 | 4 | 2 |
| C | 3 | 7 | 4 | 2 |
| D | 3 | 8 | 4 | 2 |

Cell membership is computed from the existing exact audit bin functions after canonical prompt serialization and the frozen solver's first-solution pass. The prompt/node pair is a prospective support envelope, not a constructor guarantee or a solver tuning target. No variable/value reordering, changed bin edge, alternate solver, template, favorable-signature choice, padding, or silent discard is allowed.

The source quota is exactly 1,000 accepted unique groups, balanced 250 per cell and fixed by profile as follows. Quotas apply across all source hash splits and count only a first-seen canonical-content group that is oracle-unique, scientifically eligible, inside the support envelope, and assigned to that source profile/cell. A profile/cell closes at its quota; thereafter it is removed from the active list.

| Source profile | A | B | C | D | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `eq` | 84 | 84 | 83 | 83 | 334 |
| `neq` | 83 | 83 | 84 | 83 | 333 |
| `add_mod` | 83 | 83 | 83 | 84 | 333 |

The development profiles remain `eq/neq`, `eq/add_mod`, and `neq/add_mod`. The one probe targets exactly 25 accepted eligible envelope groups per cell **in the `development_validation` split** for each pair: 100 per profile and 300 total. Development diagnostic-test records are retained for integrity/replay, cannot count toward these quotas, and provide no candidate-level or aggregate choice input. Final tasks are never sampled. A partition stops at quota closure or 30,000 candidate indices, whichever occurs first; no post-closure candidates are drawn.

Source groups retain the existing `dwr-id-split-v1` content-hash assignment to `source_train`/`source_validation`/`source_test` at buckets `<80`, `<90`, and otherwise. Development groups retain `dwr-dev-split-v1` structure-signature assignment to `development_validation` below bucket 80 and `development_diagnostic_test` otherwise. Content, lineage, and structure identities use the existing v1 canonicalizer and exact byte/domain conventions. The first unique content occurrence owns the ID; duplicate content is excluded from numerator and denominator. Lineage and development-signature split closure are independently checked over the probe. No split threshold, hash domain, split assignment, or candidate seed is inferred from outcomes.

## Candidate stream and draw order

The probe has identities `dwr-generator-v4-probe`, `dwr-constraint-development-probe-v4`, seed derivation `sha256-prefix64-v4`, and outcome schema `dwr-v4-probe-outcome-v1`. For each partition, candidate indices start at zero and increase once per attempted candidate. Derive its private stream as:

`SHA256(b"dwr-generator-v4-probe\0" + root_seed.to_bytes(8,"big") + b"\0" + partition.encode("ascii") + b"\0" + candidate_index.to_bytes(8,"big"))[:8]`, interpreted unsigned big-endian; initialize a fresh `random.Random()` and call `seed(stream_seed, version=2)`. Root seed is `20261007`; legal runtime is CPython 3.11 or 3.12. No module-global RNG, shared mutable stream, random choice outside that candidate stream, or hidden retry is allowed. Replay independently derives identical candidate streams.

At each index, form the active `(profile, cell)` list by preserving registered profile order (`eq`, `neq`, `add_mod` for source; `eq/neq`, `eq/add_mod`, `neq/add_mod` for development) and then cell order A, B, C, D, removing only closed cells. Select `active[index % len(active)]` before consuming RNG. An empty active list is a caller/state error, before RNG creation. Assign `n`, `g`, and `c` from that cell. Uniformly sample `g` distinct locations from ordered `v0`…`v5` without replacement and sort them.

For source `eq`, enumerate all `3^6` value tuples lexicographically over `(0,1,2)`. Retain a witness iff every value used in the six assignments appears among the selected given locations and the witness-satisfied normalized EQ pool contains at least `c` tuples. Select uniformly from this complete finite list with `rng.choice`. This is the review-approved necessary-condition filter; it does not choose EQ graphs, content IDs, solver bins, or outcomes. An empty list emits `no_source_eq_witness`. Then enumerate the complete normalized EQ pair pool in v1 order (lexicographic combinations of `v0`…`v5`, including anchor-anchor pairs) and retain every witness-satisfied pair. Build the same ordered active-kind count-vector list used by the other profiles for fixed total `c`; for source EQ it is the singleton `[(c,0,0)]`. Call `rng.choice` on that singleton (this call is part of the stream), then call `rng.sample(eq_pool, c)` without replacement and canonically sort the constraints. There is no independent total draw and no alternative selection path. Config `source_eq_constraint_draw` pins these calls and their order.

For source `neq`, enumerate the same lexicographic witness list, retaining assignments using all three values overall and at least two distinct values on the selected givens, then select uniformly. Enumerate the normalized unordered `neq` pairs in v1 order, retain witness-satisfied pairs touching at least one unknown, and sample exactly `c` distinct pairs uniformly without replacement. If the witness set is empty emit `no_neq_witness`; if the pool has fewer than `c`, emit `no_feasible_neq_constraint_total`. No fallback or resampling.

For source `add_mod` and all three development profiles, draw six independent uniform values in variable order. For each active kind in exact order `eq`, `neq`, `add_mod`, enumerate its complete normalized v1 tuple pool in its existing order, retaining tuples satisfied by the private witness. Anchor-anchor tuples remain in every pool. Enumerate count vectors with active-kind counts positive, inactive-kind counts zero, each count bounded by that kind's pool capacity, total exactly `c`, in lexicographic order of active counts. Select uniformly among this finite vector list, then for each active kind in kind order sample its count of distinct tuples uniformly without replacement from that kind's ordered pool. If the vector list is empty emit `no_feasible_constraint_vector` with the sampled witness only. Canonically sort the selected constraints with the existing serializer. This pins count-vector and tuple RNG calls; no constraint-total draw is made separately.

Set givens to the selected private witness values. Check the constructed task using the existing independent exhaustive oracle, which enumerates the full assignment space independently of generator/verifier and caps its uniqueness count at two. The witness must satisfy the task; an oracle count of zero is fatal `oracle_witness_disagreement`. A count of two emits `oracle_non_unique`. Run the existing deterministic solver without changing variable order (`v0`…`v5`), value order (`0,1,2`), canonical constraint order, DFS behavior, or node accounting. If its first solution differs from the unique oracle solution, abort the run as fatal `solver_oracle_disagreement`; neither disagreement is an ordinary rejection. Compute existing source single-constraint sensitivity or mixed-kind-removal and unknown-factor-connectivity evidence using the existing private generator predicates. The independent audit must recompute those interactions without importing the private predicates.

## Closed outcomes, identities, and denominators

Write exactly one UTF-8 JSONL outcome for every attempted candidate index, including proposals rejected before task construction, non-unique tasks, duplicates, ineligible unique tasks, envelope failures, and quota-accepted records. The closed outcome envelope has exactly: `outcome_version`, `partition`, `candidate_index`, `profile`, `stratum`, `n`, `given_count`, `constraint_count`, `state`, `reason`, `task_or_null`, `witness_or_null`, `oracle_solution_or_null`, `oracle_count_capped_or_null`, `solver_stats_or_null`, `content_id_or_null`, `lineage_id_or_null`, `structure_signature_or_null`, `split_or_null`, `private_eligibility_or_null`, `denominator_counted`, `prompt_utf8_bytes_or_null`, `prompt_byte_bin_or_null`, `first_solution_node_bin_or_null`, and `quota_cell_count_after_or_null`. `outcome_version` is the exact literal above; partition is `source` or `development`; candidate index is unsigned 64-bit; profile is the exact registered ordered list; stratum is `A`–`D`; n is integer 6; g/c are integers equal to the selected stratum. State is exactly `rejected_proposal`, `oracle_non_unique`, `fatal_error`, `duplicate_content`, `scientifically_ineligible`, `outside_registered_support_envelope`, `retained_diagnostic`, or `accepted_for_quota`. `reason` is the registered state-specific reason below (null only for `accepted_for_quota`). Task/witness/oracle/solver/identity/split/bin/counter fields are null until computed; `denominator_counted` is a boolean; integer count/bin fields reject booleans; hashes are lowercase 64-character SHA-256 hex. Solver statistics, when present, have exactly integer fields `first_solution_nodes`, `uniqueness_nodes`, `uniqueness_solution_count_capped` with existing bounds. Source private eligibility has exactly `{kind:"source", eligible:boolean, removal_solution_counts:[int,...]}`; mixed eligibility has exactly `{kind:"mixed", eligible:boolean, kinds:[str,...], kind_removal_solution_counts:[int,...], factor_graph_connected:boolean, factor_graph_kinds:[str,...], all_present_kinds_in_graph:boolean, reason:string|null}`. It is private audit metadata, never prompt input. No extra keys, missing keys, invalid types, or nonfinite values are accepted.

States/reasons and precedence are fixed: proposal failures use `rejected_proposal`; oracle count zero is `fatal_error/oracle_witness_disagreement`; count two is `oracle_non_unique`; solver disagreement is `fatal_error/solver_oracle_disagreement`. Fatal rows retain task, witness, exact capped oracle count (zero for witness disagreement), and null solution, solver stats, IDs, split, bins, eligibility, and quota count; the run terminates `NOT_ASSESSED` with a typed manifest error. The oracle still enumerates all assignments; only the persisted uniqueness count is capped at two. After a unique oracle result, compute canonical IDs/split and check run-wide content/lineage duplication (`duplicate_content`). For a first occurrence, insert its ID and count it in the applicable public pre-eligibility denominator, then compute and preserve prompt/node bins. Apply scientific eligibility (`source_insensitive`, or mixed `mixed_kind_not_necessary` before `mixed_unknown_factor_disconnected`); if eligible but outside `(4,2)`, use `outside_registered_support_envelope`. An eligible, in-envelope development candidate assigned to `development_diagnostic_test` uses `retained_diagnostic/development_diagnostic_split`: `denominator_counted=false`, quota count unchanged, full private outcome retained for integrity/replay, and no `accepted_records.jsonl` row. It is neither scientifically ineligible nor outside support. Only source candidates in any source split and development-validation candidates can become `accepted_for_quota`; these alone emit accepted records and increment quotas. Duplicates are never re-counted. Proposal rejects carry no task/identity/solver fields, except the two no-feasible-pool reasons retain the sampled witness; nonunique rows retain task, witness and capped count but no solution/solver/private fields. Unique first-seen rows preserve task, witness, solution, capped count, solver stats, IDs, split, private eligibility and bins. An operational failure before a candidate is complete is a terminal manifest error, never a proposal rejection.

The denominator families are: all first-seen oracle-unique source `source_train` content groups, regardless of sensitivity or envelope; and, separately for each profile, all first-seen oracle-unique `development_validation` groups, regardless of mixed eligibility or envelope. Publish source scientific sensitivity and source accepted-envelope yield separately. Publish both scientific eligibility and accepted-envelope yield separately for each development-validation profile. The conservative envelope-yield numerator is eligible **and** inside the envelope; every floor is at least 0.25 over its all-first-seen denominator. Duplicates and non-unique candidates are excluded. No diagnostic, source validation/test, or final aggregate is exposed to proposal decisions.

The private in-memory generator result is a nonserialized `ProposalResult(partition,candidate_index,profile,stratum,n,g,c,proposal_reason_or_null,task_or_null,witness_or_null,oracle_count_capped_or_null,oracle_solution_or_null,solver_stats_or_null,private_eligibility_or_null)`. Only orchestration creates the final outcome row after identities, split, denominator, bins and terminal reason are known. This internal result has no diagnostic/quota-pending terminal state and is not itself a closed artifact schema.

An accepted-record object has exactly: `record_version`, `generator_id`, `partition`, `candidate_index`, `profile`, `task`, `content_id`, `lineage_id`, `structure_signature`, `split`, `witness`, `solution`, `solver_stats`, `model_input`, and `model_input_sha256`. The first two values are `dwr-v4-probe-accepted-record-v1` and `dwr-generator-v4-probe`; identity hashes are lowercase SHA-256; profile/split/task/assignment/stat fields use the existing v1 shapes and types; `model_input` is the exact v1 projection string and its hash is computed over UTF-8 bytes. The projection contains only rules, constraints/givens and fixed answer instruction. It MUST exclude the witness as an answer label, solution, oracle results, solver results, eligibility, identities, split, stratum, quota state, or audit metadata. Artifact audit data remain separate from the model input and are never passed to the prompt encoder.

## Floors and source-before-target gate

The four exact cells are the only permissible support cells for this v4 candidate. After source quota closure, source-prefix replay and independent source audit, report only permitted `source_train` facts. Require: at least 100 accepted source-train groups; at least 12 accepted source-train structure signatures; each source primitive represented; each solution value fraction at least 0.1; at least two distinct source-train given counts; source sensitivity at least 0.25 on all first-seen unique source-train groups; at least one sensitive accepted group per accepted source-train signature; source accepted-envelope yield at least 0.25 on the same complete source-train denominator; at least ten accepted groups in each of the four registered cells; and at least 0.8 of eligible source-train groups in those four cells. This fixed four-cell capacity check is a necessary source condition, not a target support pass. If any source closure, replay, floor, or capacity check fails, do not generate development candidates.

After source passes, sample development until its three validation cell quotas close or its candidate cap is reached. The independent audit reports permitted validation-only facts: exactly 25 accepted groups/cell/profile (100/profile); at least 12 eligible signatures/profile; the unchanged at-least-one-eligible-example-per-represented-eligible-signature rule; each profile's scientific eligibility fraction at least 0.25 on its all-first-seen validation denominator; each profile's eligible-and-envelope fraction at least 0.25 on that same denominator; and every registered cell represented. Also require unchanged source-train floors above. Compute the four-cohort proxy on accepted source-train eligible groups plus the three primary-eligible development-validation pair cohorts, each cell at least ten per cohort and at least 0.8 of each cohort in those four cells. Diagnostic splits remain integrity/replay-only. This proxy is named `PASS_DEVELOPMENT_PROPOSAL` on complete success; it does not mean `PASS_FEASIBILITY`, `PASS_G2`, or full five-cohort coverage.

All original v2.2/v3 numeric floors remain binding in any future formal full run: source partition minima of 100 groups and 12 structure signatures; source ID train/validation/test minima 100/20/20; source-train 12 signatures, all primitives, solution fractions >=0.1, >=2 given-count values, sensitivity >=0.25 and one sensitive group per accepted signature; per mixed profile >=100 eligible groups and 12 eligible signatures; mixed eligibility fraction >=0.25 on unique-content oracle-unique pre-eligibility denominators; one eligible example per represented eligible signature; development validation/diagnostic totals >=80/20 and per-profile >=20/5; and actual five-cohort common support with >=10 groups/cell/cohort and >=0.8 mass in each cohort. No floor is replaced, relaxed, rounded, or inferred from an empty cohort. Probe checks do not establish full-mode facts, and full mode is outside this schema.

## Privacy and decision boundary

Before source closure, consume source outcomes/records for deterministic replay and source-quota closure only; do not publish or inspect source validation/test counts, task details, solver statistics, eligibility summaries, prompt/node histograms, or common support. After source closure, expose only the explicitly listed source-train checks to the gate. For development generation, rows assigned to `development_diagnostic_test` are saved and independently replayed for integrity only. Do not emit their aggregates or task-level records to the decision layer. Final partition is never opened or constructed. Test access is zero; no test result is adapted to. This privacy boundary applies to logs, exception text, temporary summaries and user-facing reports as well as aggregate JSON.
