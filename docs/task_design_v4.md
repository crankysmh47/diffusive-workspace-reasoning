## Arithmetic and evidence checks

The frozen source quota matrix sums by profile to `eq=334`, `neq=333`, `add_mod=333`, by cell to 250 each, and overall to 1,000. The development probe requires `3 profiles × 4 cells × 25 = 300` validation groups, 100/profile. Candidate caps permit at most 30,000 source plus 30,000 development attempts; this is a cap, not a sample-size promise. The registered resource limits are one worker, `900` combined active wall seconds and `1,073,741,824` RSS bytes. The separately conditional full limit is `2,400` seconds; maximum across those two distinct stages would be 3,300 seconds, never transferable between stages.

The reviewed graph-only capacity rows from the saved JSON give EQ valued-content upper bounds 207, 399, 333 and 204 against quotas 84, 84, 83 and 83. This is exhaustive graph arithmetic over six vertices, includes anchor-anchor edges, excludes envelope/hash effects, and is not a generated-task experiment. The assessment's old source histogram and v3 timing/RSS measurements motivated the bounded scope but are historical and do not estimate v4 constructor yield or resource use. No hidden aggregate was read for this decision.

## Unresolved scientific and operational unknowns

The one probe must determine, without redesign: whether the fixed EQ/NEQ/ADD quotas can be filled by eligible unique content; whether independent source and mixed profiles have enough distinct canonical families/signatures; whether the support envelope co-occurs with uniqueness and eligibility; whether source-train hash allocation passes its public floors and four-cell capacity check; whether three pair profiles can produce 25 eligible validation groups in every cell with the required distinct signatures; whether historical content/structure overlap affects novelty; and whether complete generation/replay/audit/finalization fits 900 seconds and 1 GiB. These are unmeasured, and a source EQ graph capacity bound does not settle them.

A null quota, floor, support, denominator, resource, or independent-audit result blocks advancement. An operational interruption is `NOT_ASSESSED`, not a mathematical NO-GO. The proposal receives one attempt; preserved negatives remain evidence. Even PASS_DEVELOPMENT_PROPOSAL establishes only the registered development proposal proxy. Full data, G2, and every training claim remain conditional on their later independently reviewed gates.
