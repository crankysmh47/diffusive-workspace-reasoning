## 1. Where the project started

The original idea was to give a frozen language model an external continuous workspace in which to reason before producing its answer. Instead of demanding that every intermediate step become text, a learned computation block would repeatedly update a collection of latent vectors. The final decoder would read that workspace through a newly trained interface. The working name, Diffusive Workspace Reasoning, reflected a possible later diffusion/flow direction; the initial implementation was always supposed to establish answer-supervised recurrence first.

The proposed axes were iteration versus one pass; joint workspace updates versus sequential latent computation; interchangeable versus indexed slots; and eventually a generative or distilled refinement mechanism. Sudoku was an early candidate task because answers can be verified. The project began with a Python package bootstrap, a large v2 research narrative and provisional model settings, rather than an implemented model or experimental result.

The owner then selected **transferable computation with exchangeable workspaces** as the main research direction. Exchangeability remained the central architectural idea; the contribution we seek is evidence that one trained workspace/interface can reuse learned computation on task compositions absent from source training, with no target-specific retraining. This is a hypothesis, not something permutation symmetry itself guarantees. Fixed-size transfer comes first; variable slot counts are a supporting study afterward.

## 2. What slots mean, and the proposed architecture

Slots are latent scratch-space vectors. They are not blank textual reasoning blocks, predefined steps, or one guaranteed slot per variable or constraint. They begin as independent random vectors and can acquire different contents as computation proceeds. A shared reasoning block updates the entire workspace using the residual equation `Z_next = Z + F(LN(Z), X)`.

An exchangeable workspace has no fixed positional identities: permuting its slots should permute the updates, while the decoder's output should remain unchanged. This still allows different slots to specialize by content. Deleting or replacing a slot is a different intervention from permuting it. The indexed control uses learned initial identities, added once, while preserving matched noise, update logic and decoder access. Its extra parameters must be reported.

The provisional fixed-size prototype is N=64, d=768 and K=4, with a frozen 1.5B–2B decoder-only backbone and newly trainable set cross-attention interfaces/projections. These are planning values, not validated launch settings. N controls workspace capacity; K controls the number of recurrent updates. The original backbone weights remain frozen, but decoder computations must retain the gradients needed by the inserted trainable interface and workspace. Prompt-only X must exclude answers and trainable adapters. The starting objective is final-answer cross-entropy.

The primary contrast is EX minus IDX at fixed N and K on macro-averaged valid-solution rate over registered held-out compositions. A frozen backbone reference, a separately trained K=0 noise-memory interface control and EX K=1 are required to interpret gains. Feedforward controls are required for recurrence-efficiency claims; sequential controls are required for joint-over-sequential claims. Equal training data, equal training compute and equal inference compute are distinct protocols, not automatically simultaneous matches.

Variable-N experiments remain staged: fixed-N transfer; then one shared checkpoint trained across registered slot counts and evaluated at seen/unseen N with K controlled; only later input-adaptive allocation or slot creation/pruning. Slot permutation, input-variable symmetry, unseen N and adaptive allocation require different evidence.

## 3. Research planning and discipline established

The repository now has a binding [v2.2 research protocol](research_protocol.md), [active transfer direction](transfer_research_direction.md), [AGENTS instructions](../AGENTS.md) and one live [checklist](../todo.md). The original v2 narrative is retained with amendment notices. The research-readiness review corrected several ambiguities: tiny Sudoku is an optional correctness fixture, logging and resume checks precede training, controls cannot wait until a favorable result, and negative/inconclusive runs are research evidence worth retaining.

Every scientific run needs a resolved manifest before execution: source/config/data/model identities, environment, independent RNG streams, precision/determinism settings, budgets and exact command. Production/confirmatory freezes require clean source. Artifacts use unique directories, stable UTF-8 serialization and SHA-256 hashes. Checkpoints must eventually include optimizer/scheduler/scaler, all RNG states and sampler position; a seed alone does not guarantee bitwise replay.

Unknown configuration keys, malformed shapes, nonfinite values and incompatible resumes must fail. No silent truncation, model/dtype substitution, skipped examples, detached recurrent gradients or weaker verification is allowed. Every changed scientific population gets a prospective version and explicit rationale. Test data are frozen; accesses are logged; adaptation to test feedback makes later results exploratory. Failed, interrupted, diverged and negative runs are preserved, with finite diagnostic/retry allowances.

Confirmatory model work requires a registered hypothesis, primary metric/contrast, practical effect size, absolute transfer/ID guardrails, checkpoint rule, sample/seed precision argument, budgets and stop rule. At least three training seeds and family-aware uncertainty are minimum requirements, not proof of statistical power. No such model study has run.

The earlier [prior-work review](research_review.md) identified overlaps with latent recurrence, frozen-decoder interfaces and set/slot architectures. It is a starting map, not an exhaustive novelty assessment. We have not established a top-tier-paper contribution. The plausible novelty is a well-controlled reusable-computation/transfer result and, if supported later, useful capacity scaling without target tuning.

## 4. Task and CPU infrastructure implemented

The active task grammar is finite-domain constraint completion over values {0,1,2}: EQ(a,b), NEQ(a,b), and ADD_MOD(a,b;c), meaning `(a+b) mod3 = c`. Tasks include variables, constraints and fixed givens; the answer is one complete satisfying assignment. Source instances use individual primitives; development uses the three pair compositions; the reserved final profile uses all three. Explicit rule definitions are shared across model inputs; definitions of a rule do not count as training instances of a held-out composition.

Implementation under `dwr/tasks/` includes strict immutable schemas, normalized serialization, a JSON answer verifier with stable failure precedence, exhaustive tiny-instance solution enumeration, a deterministic depth-first reference solver and solver statistics. Generator and verifier logic are cross-checked independently. Zero/one/multiple-solution cases, legal operand roles, fixed givens and annotation exclusion are tested.

Canonical content IDs account for variable renaming and legal operand symmetries; structure signatures omit values while retaining the relevant anchors/structure. Source ID splits are content-based; development validation/diagnostic splits are structure-based; the final composition is separately held out. Versioned seeded generators, global content ownership, lineage checks, every-attempt ledgers, persisted replay, protected-split routing and independent corpus audits have been built. None of these synthetic split checks establishes absence from a pretrained model's training corpus.

Source eligibility requires actual constraint sensitivity; mixed eligibility requires every present kind to be necessary, a connected unknown-variable factor graph and every kind's participation. Matching also uses fixed size, given count, constraint count, prompt length and reference-solver node bins. These measures support controlled comparisons; solver effort and weak-propagation resistance are not universal reasoning difficulty.

Windows work addressed bounded atomic-file replacement conflicts, resource accounting and a retained ctypes/pointer-cache issue in repeated memory monitoring. Exact canonicalization was optimized by comparing normalized tuples across the same exhaustive permutations and serializing only the winning permutation. Equivalence was checked against the original algorithm on 4,509 exhaustive three-variable tasks plus supported-size fixtures. The isolated single fixture measurement moved from approximately0.438 to0.345 seconds; this is not a production-scale or statistical speedup claim.

## 6. Current rank-certified design and unfinished implementation

The next [prospective design](certificate_rank_design.md), committed as `2858750`, uses finite F3 rank/nullspace construction to make hard ADD-containing pair tasks unique algebraically while preserving each kind-deletion alternative. EQ/ADD uses a full-rank mixed linear basis; NEQ/ADD uses a rank-(m-1) ADD basis and inequalities excluding the other two points of its three-point solution fiber. Padding and connectivity remain explicit conditions, with every failure consuming its index. Existing source and ordinary development branches remain versioned delegates.

Independent hand review found a limitation: with three unknowns, the final triple profile cannot be unique and all-three-kind-necessary under the same no-unary rule. The frozen final grammar therefore permits unary constraints and uses cross-certificate pools plus a rank-deficient linear fiber. That creates a locality distinction which must be reported; it does not prove empirical final capacity. Final implementation is still pending; the current `construct(..., partition="final")` raises NotImplementedError.

The registered qualification uses root20261014,3,600 source/4,800 development/final0, one worker,1GiB and1,800 active seconds, with zero retries. Only a PASS permits the independently seeded full study: root20261015, minimum1,000 source A,600 A per development pair and600 final A, at most30,000 raw attempts per partition and3,600 active seconds. Full G2 still requires all five cohorts, including source ID-test and the reserved final composition, to satisfy fixed support/leakage/eligibility/diversity floors. This registration has not been executed.

Eight newly written source/test files are preserved as WIP: `f3.py`, `certificate_rank_generator.py`, `certificate_rank_audit.py`, `certificate_rank_study.py` and their four test files. ## 8. What remains before a research paper

First finish and independently review the rank constructor/runner, including the final grammar and meaningful fixture/failure checks; then freeze clean source and execute its single registered qualification. Only PASS enables the predeclared full corpus and five-cohort G2 audit. Scientific or resource failures must be preserved rather than repaired by unregistered seed/threshold searches.

After full G2 PASS, implement fixed-N EX/IDX tensors and the decoder interface; verify permutation behavior, causal masks, frozen-weight immutability, gradient flow, resume and full memory/cost. Select a pinned backbone and complete non-null model preregistration, then run development pilots and the matched multi-seed transfer study. Variable N, allocation, cross-family claims and flow/distillation remain subsequent work.

The project has substantial task/reproducibility infrastructure and useful negative design evidence. It has no trained workspace, EX-versus-IDX result, demonstrated transfer, diffusion result or validated publication claim. A percentage of paper completion would be misleading: the central architectural hypothesis has not yet been tested.
