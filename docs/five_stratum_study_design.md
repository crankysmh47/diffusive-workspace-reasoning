## Rationale, departure and claim

The old4..7-node envelope systematically excluded stalled development examples. The retained complete140-cell inventory has five, and only five, cells with at least10 E in every permitted source/development cohort. Adopt all five jointly populated cells as a newly frozen support definition. This is a substantive envelope/population/estimand change, not a reporting correction, proof that the original gate was wrong or a retroactive PASS. It uses allowed training/validation feedback; the original test/protected task payloads and final composition remained unavailable for choices.

The diagnosis prohibited optimizing an envelope or automatically advancing a population. That prohibition remains satisfied: it emitted all bins, no selected set or new gate. Root now makes a separately dated design decision using the predeclared joint-capacity rule, followed by fresh replication. Retained coverage arithmetic is hypothesis-generating, not acceptance evidence. No additional cell is chosen by maximizing coverage or by inspecting final/test outcomes; the frozen set cannot change after this registration. If the fresh gate fails, preserve it and stop this proposal without another seed, prefix extension or favorable subset.

The alternatives rejected are a globally widened solver cutoff (which would pool sparse g3 high-node strata), reducing the hard branch just to improve coverage, or constructing yet another task law when a supported hard regime already exists. Constructor laws, canonical identities, global deduplication, splits, prompts, independent verification and hard/ordinary allocation remain identical to rank-v1. The claim is transfer on this supported finite grammar with accompanying locality/GAC shifts, not a pure composition-only causal effect, uniform feasible-task sampling or general mathematical reasoning.

Primary future EX-IDX contrast is the difference in equally weighted composition-macro means of this metric, with paired examples/seeds and family-aware uncertainty. Final composition set is fixed before model registration; the intended held-out three pair diagnostic-test groups plus triple-final group have equal composition weights. Source-ID reporting and development validation also report each fine stratum explicitly. An empty stratum makes that metric undefined; it cannot be silently omitted or renormalized.

Training mixture/data exposure and cost matching are later preregistered consistently across methods; equal evaluation weights are not evidence of balanced training exposure.
